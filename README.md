## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Nimbit Frontier**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for exploration, resource gathering, creature care, and Underwild combat. It is designed for players who want to reduce routine grinding while preserving the game's habitat, conservation, town, and dungeon systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who enjoy Nimbit Frontier's creature care, habitat design, conservation, town life, and exploration but want less resource grinding or a more relaxed approach to Underwild combat.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game first, load a save, and activate the trainer after the player character becomes controllable. Use the movement options to travel through Star's End and the Underwild more quickly. Disable speed-related options before entering scripted scenes, shops, or transition points.

### Tools & Inventory

Use inventory options after opening the inventory or selecting the relevant item. Unlimited item and resource functions are best limited to ordinary supplies and should be disabled before completing tutorials, deliveries, or quest hand-ins if an item count does not update correctly.

### World & Environment

Resource and gold options support habitat construction, home upgrades, fishing, foraging, museum donations, League progression, and sanctuary improvements. Save normally after making major purchases or releasing Nimbits, and avoid changing values during loading screens or cinematic events.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate combat assistance before entering an Underwild dive. God Mode protects against hazards and Mimock attacks, while fast weakening and tether assistance shorten the process of collecting genetic fragments. Disable combat options when leaving the dungeon or during scripted encounters to reduce the chance of progression issues.
